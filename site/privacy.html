Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated 30 October 2026

This website

  • No tracking. The site has no analytics, no advertising, and sets no cookies. It stores nothing in your browser.
  • No third parties. Everything the page needs, including its fonts and the logos, is served from this site, so loading it does not send your visit to anyone else.
  • The early-access form is the only thing we collect. If you submit it, we receive your email address and, if you chose one, the plan you were interested in. They are sent to the service we use to receive form submissions.

What we do with your email

We use it to contact you about Unbroke early access and product updates, and for nothing else. We don't sell it or share it for advertising. We keep it until you ask us to remove it.

Remove your email or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/yugsoni21/unbroke/issues titled "Remove my email", without putting your address in it, and we will get in touch to confirm and remove it.

The Unbroke tool itself

  • Scanning reads your source files on your own machine and never runs them. It does not send your code, or any usage data, anywhere. There is no telemetry.
  • It downloads public API specifications (from GitHub and the providers) to compare them.
  • Your code leaves your machine in only one case: if you run the fix agent, the affected files are sent to the model service you configured with your own key. You can preview exactly what would be sent first.

The README lists where the tool stores files on your computer.
